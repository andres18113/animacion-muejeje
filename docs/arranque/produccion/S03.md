## Retrospective

The transformation hold, animated sum from the hand and physical path through the chips worked especially well. The audit found timing differences absent from the original tests and background-dependent contrast issues. After three corrections, the reading strategy changed to dedicated opaque surfaces; the last finding required continuing the listeners' environmental clock without changing the mascots or S02. Transferable lessons:

- Numerical continuity does not detect an omitted visual layer: compare state with the drawn component.
- Execution must wait for visible reception; sharing parameters between script and packets prevents contradictory timelines.
- Measure contrast on the actual composite during the anchor words; artistic transparency does not guarantee readability.
- Projected labels need a stable reading surface when passing over light hardware.
- Listeners' procedural clocks must continue across the cut; the cue clock remains local.
- Reviewing anchor/cut/sequence PNGs and measuring contrast first allows the version to be frozen before spending the full render.
