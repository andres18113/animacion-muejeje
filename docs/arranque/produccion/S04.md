## Technical check

Eight contiguous 64KiB blocks with internal offsets, never CPU addresses: B0 0x00000–0x0FFFF backup; B1 0x10000–0x1FFFF fault tolerance; B2–B6 0x20000–0x6FFFF main BIOS; B7 0x70000–0x7FFFF Boot Block. B1 contains two 8KiB parameter subblocks and 48KiB reserved region. The display is an educational section; it does not represent writing or erasing during boot. Flash retains firmware without a battery; RTC/CMOS retains configuration and clock with backup power.

Cmosi is anchored to PIIX4E `[1.4,.12,3.6]`, not the battery `[-1.6,.12,-.2]`. Supplementary confirmation: Intel 82371EB datasheet, p16 MC146818A-compatible RTC and 256B backed RAM, p43 battery-powered RTC plane; copy consulted by explorer at https://www.ardent-tool.com/datasheets/Intel_82371EB.pdf .

## Final retrospective

The physical eight-block section, close shots and material RTC linked to its battery worked well. Reviewer detected occlusions, a static panel, clipping and confusion between readable annotation and physical instruction front. Cause: solving text and framing without checking all actors or preserving path semantics. After three reviews architecture changed: small carrier on actual track and explanation linked to hand with dashed leader.

Lessons: reserve reading regions per shot and check each element (including battery/caption), measure actual silhouette as well as nominal box and distinguish physical data from its annotations. References require the same capture protocol: do not compare an approved batch with a cold still. Exit codes and structured results take precedence over a log tail.
