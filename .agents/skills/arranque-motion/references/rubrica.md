# Critique and correction rubric

Use this list after numerical validation and rendering. Mark every item **PASS/FAIL** with evidence (capture/video path, frame/interval, command result and observation). Do not approve based on overall impression or turn a numerical test into visual acceptance. Correct failures, repeat affected checks and retain evidence of the final version. Claude Code directs, reviews and validates.

1. **Accuracy and baseline.** Every beat matches a requirement and operation in the historical specification `SO-ARR-BIOS-001 v2.0.0.md`, §§1, 4, 8, 9 and 12, and [ContratoEscena](repo:src/arranque/escenas.ts#ContratoEscena). The external specification is optional authoring context and is not included; use local contracts and `tests/fixtures/boot-contract.json` for clone checks. Legacy BIOS, 32-bit Pentium II, PC100 SDRAM, IDE/ATA, 512 KiB parallel flash, MBR and GRUB Legacy; do not introduce UEFI, GPT, NVMe or incompatible generations. Teaching abstractions are identified as such. Data, binary values and addresses match the contract. Pass if there is no technical contradiction or omitted requirement.
2. **Causality.** Viewers identify who requests, who responds, what travels and why state changes. Signals follow actual traces/wires in [PISTAS](repo:src/arranque/cine/Mundo3D.tsx#PISTAS) and [MAZO_ATX](repo:src/arranque/cine/Mundo3D.tsx#MAZO_ATX); AC, DC, pulse and level are distinguished by shape and persistence. For A01–A07: +5VSB→request→PS_ON#=0→outputs/stabilization→PWR_OK→clock/reset→CPU enabled. Pass if no effect precedes its cause and the hologram cannot be confused with an actual state that has already occurred.
3. **Cinematic language.** Every movement reveals a technical fact or necessary reaction. No beige presentation backgrounds, card grids, static panels, time labels or slide headers. Instruments integrate into the action and record events. Pass if every movement has a verifiable reason in the beat sheet.
4. **Acting.** Anticipation, action, contact and settling are readable. Listeners react and look at the correct source/destination. Arms remain approximately within ±100°, without crossing faces, hidden hands or unreadable overshoot. [Actor](repo:src/arranque/cine/Actor.tsx#Actor) retains breathing/blinking and the mouth follows visemes throughout ALL speech, including closed consonants. Pass if the sequence conveys intent and no two mouths speak at once.
5. **Camera.** Full speaker, apparent height ≥200 px and feet ≤885 when subtitles are present; review jump and arm extremes during movement. Composition retains the relationship between subject and target, depth and signal direction. Pass `node tests/validar-cine.mjs` from [validar-cine](repo:tests/validar-cine.mjs) and capture/sequence review without clipping or occlusion.
6. **Legibility and accessibility.** [SubtituloCine](repo:src/arranque/cine/SubtituloCine.tsx#SubtituloCine) shares voice/mouth segments; maximum two legible lines without obscuring feet or data. Review at delivery scale and in preview. As a WCAG review criterion, check effective contrast ≥4.5:1 for normal text and ≥3:1 for large text against the actual composited background; do not assume a shadow guarantees it. Do not communicate through color alone: retain shape, trajectory, technical label or state. Avoid uncomfortable flashing; distinguish the represented clock from excessive visual blinking. Pass if readability, contrast and duration allow understanding data during its mention. These thresholds are rubric criteria, not a claim that the spec enumerates WCAG.
7. **Continuity.** Camera and shared characters retain state at the cut; persistent signals remain present, with coherent phase. For new cuts, last visible N = first N+1; preserve the exact historical S01→S02 rules in the [validator](repo:tests/validar-cine.mjs). Review at least the two adjacent frames and a surrounding sequence. Pass if there is no unexplained teleportation, power-off or jump.
8. **Synchronization.** [ANCLAS_VISUALES](repo:src/arranque/anclas.ts#ANCLAS_VISUALES) remains strictly ordered; events/data appear during the corresponding word (§9.3). Distinguish signal start and reception when synchronizing [Trazas](repo:src/arranque/cine/instrumentos.tsx#Trazas). No voice/voice overlap; [detectarSolapes](repo:src/arranque/audio.ts#detectarSolapes) only allows SFX over voice with explicit per-track permission. Pass `node tests/validar-h02.mjs` from [validar-h02](repo:tests/validar-h02.mjs) and review with actual audio when available. Without recording, report “estimated synchronization”.
9. **Determinism and performance.** [crearMotor](repo:src/arranque/cine/motor.ts#crearMotor) returns the same state when frames are requested in different orders; the same PNG rendered twice retains SHA-256 on the same machine/GPU/configuration. Reuse the bundle, preview `--scale=0.5 --concurrency=2`, and record render duration/errors; do not claim measured performance without measurement. Pass if sequences and preview finish without failed frames and approved regression retains hashes.
10. **Regression and delivery.** Pass every mandatory command with exit 0: cine, h02, rigs, mascot verification, SVG validation, TypeScript, ESLint and skill validator; attach render plan, indexes, manifests and review evidence. [Root.tsx](repo:src/Root.tsx) retains CRLF. S01/S02 retain approved pixels unless a visual change is requested and approved; updating hashes never replaces explaining a difference. Pass if there are no FAIL items, pending evidence or renders presented as executed when only `--dry-run` was prepared.

Record format:

| Item | PASS/FAIL | Evidence + frame | Defect / correction | Final review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–10 | One row per item | Path and measured command | Concrete action or “no defect” | Date and reviewer |

If a check requires the host and has not run yet, mark **PENDING**, list the command and do not claim approved visual delivery. The sandbox cannot run Remotion CLI because of `esbuild: Access is denied`; do not bypass it with internal bundler/renderer imports.
